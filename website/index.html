Just bring standard indoor athletic wear: a t-shirt, shorts, and a good pair of indoor court shoes (volleyball, basketball, or indoor soccer shoes work best). Don't forget a water bottle! We provide all the handballs and training equipment.
Yes! Your first practice is completely free. We want you to come out, meet the team, and see if you enjoy the sport before you pay anything. Just show up to one of our scheduled practices and introduce yourself to a coach or captain.
Our weekly practices are co-ed, meaning everyone trains together. However, when we travel to official tournaments (like CalCup or US Nationals), we compete in separate Men's and Women's divisions. We always send a Men's team, and for the Women's division, we either send our own team or join forces with other regional clubs to ensure everyone gets to compete!
